# Project Brief

## Working name

Markellos Professional Apps

## Intended domain

`professional.markellosecosystem.com`

## Purpose

Create a focused professional portfolio presenting Markellos Markides' experience at the intersection of civil engineering, project management, contract management, health and safety, public-sector operations and responsible digital process improvement.

## Primary audience

- professional contacts and collaborators;
- managers and technical decision-makers;
- public-sector and engineering professionals;
- organisations interested in structured process improvement;
- software and product professionals reviewing the case-study approach.

## Core outcomes

Visitors should understand:

1. the professional foundation behind the work;
2. the operational problems addressed;
3. the role played in analysis, system design and delivery;
4. the safeguards applied to privacy, security and professional responsibility;
5. how to make contact for a professional discussion.

## Initial information architecture

- Hero and positioning
- Professional profile
- Areas of expertise
- Three anonymised case studies
- Working approach
- Confidentiality statement
- Contact

## Initial case studies

- Health & Safety Management Application
- Electronic Treasury Workflow
- Monthly Service Roster Tool

## Languages

English and Greek, with equivalent meaning rather than literal word-for-word translation.

## Non-goals

This website is not:

- an operational portal for the private applications;
- a public copy of private repositories;
- an official website of any employer, authority or public organisation;
- a source of legal, safety, financial or administrative authority;
- a place for real operational, personal or confidential data.
